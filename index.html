Roots

Grandparent stories, in both languages. A grandparent speaks or writes in their own language, and their grandchild gets a storybook they can actually understand — the original and the translation side by side, with the care and the idioms explained, not just translated word for word.

Everything you type or record stays in this browser unless you add a model key below. Nothing is uploaded to a server by this page on its own.

1. The story or message

Recording stays on this device and is only played back here. If your browser supports it, you can also use its dictation to fill the box above.

With a key, Muse Spark translates with cultural context and explains idioms, and Muse Image illustrates each page. Without a key, the output-language side stays editable for you to fill in, and illustrations show a placeholder — the book still works.

2. Your book

Illustration appears here with a model key — otherwise this is a placeholder page.

Roots is a family storybook, not a medical, legal, or professional translation service. Check any translation that matters before relying on it. A demo example in this app is fictional — never put a real child's private story or photo into a public submission.
