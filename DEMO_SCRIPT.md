# Roots — 90-second demo video script

**Goal:** one real book made, start to finish, in 90 seconds. No API key
required for this take — the book works without one, and that is part of
the story. If the $150 credit key arrives before recording, shoot a second
take with live Spark translation and Muse Image illustrations and use
whichever take is cleaner.

**Privacy rule for this video:** use the fictional example story already
in the app. Do NOT use a real child's story, voice recording, or photo in
a public submission video unless you have explicitly decided to share it.

## Script (about 180 words spoken, ~90 seconds)

### 0:00–0:10 — The problem (face to camera, or title card)
> "My kids live in San Jose. My parents live in Chennai. When my little
> one broke his arm, my parents poured out their care in Tamil — and my
> kids heard the love in their voices, but missed the words. Roots fixes
> that."

*On screen: title card "Roots — the words, and the warmth, across the
world."*

### 0:10–0:25 — The story goes in (screen recording, Roots app open)
> "Grandma types her story in Tamil — or records it. Nothing leaves the
> browser."

*On screen: click "Load a fictional example", show the Tamil text.
Mention the recording button, but do not record a real voice for the
public video.*

### 0:25–0:45 — The book comes out
> "Press Make my book, and it splits into pages. Tamil on every page,
> English beside it — editable, so a parent can fix anything."

*On screen: click "Make my book". Flip from page 1 to page 2. Edit one
English line live to show it is editable.*

### 0:45–1:05 — Hear it, and understand the care
> "Tap to hear it in Tamil, then in English. And this is the part a
> dictionary misses — 'paravaillai, kavalaipadadhe' isn't just 'it's
> okay, don't worry.' It's a grandmother saying: I'm here, you're safe."

*On screen: click "Hear in Tamil", then "Hear in English". Show the
idiom/culture note on the page (with a key) or say the line above over
the Tamil text (without a key).*

### 1:05–1:20 — Illustrations (only if a key is available)
> "With a model key, Muse Image illustrates every page in one
> storybook style. Without one, the book still works — the story is the
> point, not the pictures."

*On screen: illustration loading on a page, or the placeholder page if
there is no key. Do not fake an illustration.*

### 1:20–1:30 — Keep it and close
> "Save it in the browser, download it, and send it to the family.
> Roots — because a grandchild should understand their grandmother."

*On screen: click "Download book as a file", then the closing title
card with the app name. End.*

## Production notes

- **Record:** screen-record the browser at 1280×720 or higher, with
  system audio on for the tap-to-hear moment. Captions on, always.
- **Length:** if the take runs over 95 seconds, cut the illustration
  section first — the translation and tap-to-hear moments are the demo.
- **No key, no problem:** the no-key take is the honest one. Say so on
  camera ("no key needed") rather than pretending a live call happened.
- **What not to show:** a real API key, a real child's name, photo, or
  voice, or any real family story — the fictional example is the demo.
- **Upload:** add the finished video link to `README.md` and to the
  Cerebral Valley submission form before Wed Nov 4, 6:00 PM PT.
